# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing views, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, trolling, publication of private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the security contact or another private channel published by the project. Maintainers will investigate fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and may remove content or restrict participation.
